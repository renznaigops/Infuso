Schedule

Home / Payroll

Payroll

Generate and manage staff payroll from finalized attendance.

Staff IDNamePositionRate (₱/hr)Hours workedGross pay (₱)Deductions (₱)Net pay (₱)StatusActions

Hours come from Attendance records and Schedules, never typed in. Payroll covers the current week and is calculated live up to today, so it is ready on the payroll date (the last day of the period). Absent days are unpaid, pay is the scheduled hours on days worked (there is no overtime or undertime), and Tardiness and manual Deductions are subtracted. Statuses move Draft → For review → Approved → Processed → Released. Deductions sync automatically while payroll is Draft, For review or Approved. Changing deductions on an Approved record sends it back to For review. Processing locks the figures, and releasing the payslip to the staff is a separate step. Staff stay in the current period until the whole period closes and moves to History.

Closed pay periods, archived as a whole. Processed payroll is locked and no longer changes if attendance or deductions are edited later.
